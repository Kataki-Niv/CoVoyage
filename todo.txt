3. COUNTRY "GOOD TO KNOW" FACTS — REPLACE THE GENERIC/STUPID CONTENT
==================================================

The current country facts are still too generic and read like generic travel advice.

This needs a proper content-quality pass.

"Good to Know" must contain ACTUAL DISTINCTIVE FACTS about the specific country.

Do NOT use generic advice such as:
- "Respect local customs."
- "Carry cash."
- "Check the weather."
- "Learn a few local phrases."
- "Public transport can be useful."
- "Try the local food."
- "Book ahead."
- "Stay hydrated."

Those are travel tips, NOT country facts.

Instead, provide memorable, country-specific information such as:
- an unusual law or regulation
- a distinctive geographic fact
- a unique cultural tradition
- a historical fact directly connected to the country
- a distinctive language fact
- a country-specific social/customary detail
- a notable natural phenomenon
- a unique infrastructure/transport fact
- a distinctive food/cultural origin fact
- another verifiable fact that would make a traveller say "I didn't know that."

Examples of the desired LEVEL OF SPECIFICITY:

Instead of:
"France has many beautiful regions."

Use something specific such as:
"France is divided into 18 administrative regions, including overseas regions such as Réunion and Guadeloupe."

Instead of:
"Italy has a rich history."

Use something specific such as:
"Italy has more UNESCO World Heritage properties than any other country."

Instead of:
"Japan has unique traditions."

Use something specific such as:
"Japan has a formal system of 47 prefectures, with Tokyo classified as a metropolitan prefecture rather than simply a conventional city."

The exact facts must be appropriate and accurate for the country.

IMPORTANT:
- Avoid sensationalizing unusual laws or customs.
- Do not make dubious claims just because they sound interesting.
- Prefer reliable, distinctive facts.
- Each country's fact should clearly belong to THAT country.
- No repeated generic facts across all countries.
- No Japan-derived fallback facts.
- No country should inherit another country's Good to Know content.

Audit all 20 countries individually.

- Use object-cover where appropriate.
- Preserve existing borders, radius, shadows, and editorial styling.
- Preserve existing typography.
